# Generated people portraits

Generated on 2026-10-10 using the built-in ImageGen tool. All six subjects are fictional Bangladeshi adults, not photographs of UrShop staff, customers, or endorsers. Use as illustrative content only. Merchant stories and hero sales amounts are labeled as examples.

## Delivery

The website uses six local 256 × 256 WebP assets in `src/assets/people/`, rendered by `src/components/ui/person-avatar.tsx` through `next/image`. The component supplies display dimensions, a matching `sizes` hint, circular crops and empty decorative alt text. Existing adjacent names and roles provide context. Original generated PNGs remain in the image tool's output directory; they are not shipped with the site.

## Placements

- Home: hero merchant badges, overlapping hero portraits and illustrative merchant story cards.
- Features: customer-list demonstration.
- Themes: sample storefront testimonial blocks in homepage and page-builder demonstrations.
- Security: owner, operations, catalog and support team demonstration.
- Partners: agency staff demonstration.

Other pages have no profile-photo slots. Product illustrations, logos and actual author/instructor identity are retained.
